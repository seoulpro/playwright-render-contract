# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We pledge to
act in ways that keep the community open, welcoming, and respectful.

## Expected and prohibited conduct

Behavior that helps the community includes:

- showing empathy and respecting differing viewpoints and experience;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention;
- insults, derogatory comments, trolling, or personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces—issues, pull requests, code
review, and any other project channels—and when someone officially represents
the project in public spaces, such as using an official account or acting as a
designated representative.

## Project-specific expectations

Keep technical disagreement evidence-based: prefer reproductions, findings, and
references over assertions about people.

Render reports, logs, fixtures, and screenshots can contain private or sensitive
application data. Review and redact them before sharing in issues, pull
requests, or any other public space.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to
[lim@limsumin.com](mailto:lim@limsumin.com). Reports are reviewed as promptly
and fairly as practical, and the reporter's identity is kept confidential
wherever possible.

Suspected security vulnerabilities are not conduct reports: follow
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) instead, and do not send them to the conduct
address or open them as public issues.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and
may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that
do not align with this Code of Conduct. Depending on the severity and pattern of
the behavior, responses may range from a private warning to temporary
restrictions on participation or permanent exclusion from the project. Action
is proportionate to the violation, and enforcement decisions are explained when
doing so is appropriate.
